Description

Elevate your laundry experience this Black Friday with the Hisense 14kg Top Loader Washing Machine in Premium Black (Model WT3K1423UB). Designed to handle large family laundry loads effortlessly, this modern top loader combines high washing performance, water and energy efficiency, and a sleek contemporary aesthetic.

Key Features & Performance:

  • 14 kg Drum Capacity: Easily accommodates bulky bedding, heavy blankets, towels, and large family laundry loads in a single cycle.
  • iBubble & Bubble Clean Technology: Generates millions of dense micro-bubbles that activate detergent quickly, penetrating deep into fabric fibers to lift tough stains gently.
  • Smart Fuzzy Logic Control: Intelligent sensors measure the exact weight of your laundry load and automatically calculate the optimal water level and wash duration.
  • 8 Dedicated Wash Programs: Includes Normal, Quick Wash (15-min), Eco, Delicates, Intensive / Heavy, Air Dry, Spin Only, and Tub Clean.
  • Air Dry & Aqua Save: Reduces residual moisture for significantly faster drying times and optimizes water usage for enhanced efficiency.
  • Soft-Closing Tempered Glass Lid: Sleek semi-transparent glass door closes silently and safely without slamming.
  • Durable Stainless Steel Drum: Long-lasting rust-resistant tub with interior Magic Filter to capture lint and detergent residue.
  • Safety & Reliability: Child lock, auto-restart memory backup, and self-diagnostics troubleshooting.
